Core Capabilities
Everything you need for digital prescriptions
OCR Prescription Digitization
Upload any paper prescription — handwritten or printed. Our engine converts it to structured, editable data with 98%+ accuracy.
AI Medicine Extraction
LLM-powered extraction of drug names, dosages, frequencies, and durations. Automatically corrects OCR errors using medical knowledge bases.
Smart Dose Scheduling
Auto-generates dose calendars from prescription frequencies (BD, TDS, PRN). Time-aware scheduling with missed dose detection.
Real-Time Reminders
Intelligent notification system for upcoming, missed, and completed doses. Configurable reminder windows per medication.
Adherence Analytics
Visual dashboards tracking medication compliance. Adherence percentage, trend analysis, and weekly/monthly patterns.
12+ Language Support
Transliterate prescriptions to Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and more — in native script. Audio readout in Hinglish for accessibility.
